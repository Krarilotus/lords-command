---
id: rule.briefs-point-not-restate
kind: rule
title: Briefs carry the task and point to the rules
audience: [all]
roles: [orchestrator]
scope: global
status: active
visibility: public
evidence:
- summary: Rules restated in briefs drifted (one brief repeated a host rule five times); mandatory per-brief lines and report checklists added weight to every task.
  date: 2026-10-25
origin:
  type: own
topics: [prompts, delegation]
---
A brief states the task, its scope and the context it needs, and points to the rules by reference instead of restating
them. No mandatory boilerplate lines or report checklists in every brief.
